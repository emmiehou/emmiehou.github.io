food

Growing up, I was obsessed with cooking shows and genuinely dreamed of competing on MasterChef and Chopped. Food Network was my Disney Channel. Ever since moving into college and having my own kitchen to cook daily meals, cooking has been my favorite creative outlet outside of engineering. I pride myself on cooking without recipes or measurements. From making handmade pasta, slow-stirred risottos, and pan sauces to testing out new recipes and restaurant-inspired flavors, here's a snapshot of my photos album, "Good food I made."
